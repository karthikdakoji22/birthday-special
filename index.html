Vandhuu… 🤍🧿

Nenu ee message endhuku raasthunnano start cheyyadam konchem difficult ga undhi…🥹 because idi naa story kaadhu. Naa friend heart lo inni years nunchi silent gaa unna oka story ni… athanu cheppalekapoyina konni feelings ni… nee mundhu pettadaniki chesthunna oka small attempt. ❤️‍🩹

So please… idi just oka friend cheppina matter la chadavaku.

Oka abbayi life lo oka ammayi entha deep gaa oka part aipoyindho cheppe story la chaduvu. 🫂

Third class nunchi ippatI varaku… 14 years. 🥹

School corridors… chocolates… 🍫 auto varaku raavadam… pink cycle meedha wait cheyyadam… 🚲 okari kosam okaru wait cheyyadam… chinna chinna conversations… secrets share cheskovadam…

Bayata nunchi chusthe avi just school memories.
Kaani athaniki avi memories kanna ekkuva.
Avi athani childhood lo nuvvu.

Anduke konni years pass ayina… aa memories fade avvaledu.

“Konni gnapakaalu manam gurthupettukomu… avi manalni vadili vellavu.” 🤍

Nuvvu athanini school days lo ela care chesavo… athanu ippatiki aa care ni chaala special gaa gurthupettukunnadu.

Maa family taruvatha… “Maa amma laga kaadu… oka amma laga nannu care chesina person” ani feel ayyadu.

Anduke ninnu prematho “Amma” ani pilichevadu.

Adi just oka nickname kaadhu.
“Ee ammayi ilaane nannu chuskunte chaalu… inka em kavali?” ane feeling. 🥹

Oka abbayiki… thanani judge cheyyakunda care chese, athanitho comfortable gaa undey, athaniki safe gaa anipinche oka ammayi dorikithe… daaniki minchi em korukuntadu raa? ❤️‍🩹

Nuvvu okasari athanitho…

“Nee daggara nenu filter lekunda nenela untaano ala undagalanu… nee valla naaku safe ga anipisthundi”

Maybe neeku adi oka simple sentence.
Athanikaithe… adi friendship lo athanu gelichina moment. 🥹

Because someone saying “I can be myself with you” is not a small thing. Especially when that person matters to you.

And then life changed.

School days aipoyayi. People changed. Situations changed. Mee madhya distance vachindhi.

Maybe reasons unnayi… maybe circumstances unnayi… maybe time itself mee iddarini different directions lo teesukellindhi.

Kaani interesting thing enti ante… distance vachindhi conversation ki… athani mind ki kaadhu.

Nee gurinchi athanu alochinchadam aapaledu. Nee happiness… nee safety… nee family… nee future… ivanni ippatiki athani concern lo unnayi.

And most importantly—nuvvu athanitho unna lekapoyina happy ga undali ani korukuntunnadu.

“Nuvvu happy ga unnapudu nenu gurthuraakapoyina parledhu… kaani badhalo ontariga unnappudu nenu antu okadini unna ani marchipoku.” 🫂❤️‍🩹

Athani gurinchi nenu chala sarlu observe chesina oka thing undhi.

Athanu ninnu own cheskovalani anukovadam kanna… ninnu ardham cheskovalani ekkuva anukuntadu.

Nee dreams ni respect cheyyali. Nee freedom ni respect cheyyali. Nee family ni respect cheyyali. Nee problems ni ardham cheskovali. Nuvvu happy ga unte happy avvali. And nuvvu badha lo unte… “nenu unna” ani cheppagalige person ga undali.

Maybe anduke athani feeling ni just “love” ani oka word tho explain cheyyadam saripodu.

“Raatha loo leni Raadha kosame intha thapisthunnavvv… alantidhi nee life loki vachina ammayini inkentha apurupam gaa choosukuntaavo nee maatallone kanipisthundhi.” ❤️‍🩹

And honestly… athanitho matladina prathi sari naaku adhe anipisthundi.

Because relationship start avvakamundhe… care, understanding, freedom, support, respect ivanni entha important o ardham chesukunna abbayi… thanu nijanga preminchina ammayini ela choosukuntado imagine cheyyadam difficult kaadu.

Kaani ikkada oka important thing cheppali.

Nenu ninnu convince cheyyadaniki ee message raayadam ledu.

“Athanni accept cheyyi” ani kaadu.
“Athani love ki answer ivvu” ani kaadu.

Nee heart em chepthundo… adi nee choice. 🤍

Because love ni force cheyyalem. Feelings ni request cheyyalem. Oka heart ki inkoka heart meeda place create avvadam anedi manam decide chesina ventane jarigedhi kaadhu.

But… athanu ninnu ela chusthunnado neeku teliyali.

Anthe.

Anduke nee mundhu athani side of the story pettalanipinchindhi.

Because maybe… nuvvu marchipoyina oka chocolate 🍫 athaniki memory.
Nuvvu normal ga anukunna oka conversation… athaniki comfort.
Nuvvu casual ga chesina oka care… athaniki childhood lo safest feeling.
Nuvvu mic lo chesina oka birthday wish… athaniki lifetime memory. 🥹

Manam evariki entha important mo manaki chala saarlu teliyadu.

“Prema puttali ante manushulu kaadhu… manasulu kalavali ani anukunna. But nee valle thelisindhi… manushulu kalisaake manasulu kuda kalusthaayi ani.” ❤️

Mee story lo adhe strange beauty.

First childhood lo manushulu kalisaru. Taruvatha friendship perigindhi. Care perigindhi. Memories perigayi. And somewhere along the way… athani heart lo nuvvu ordinary friend nunchi extraordinary person aipoyav.

Athanu aa feeling ni exact ga eppudu realize ayyado… maybe athanikey teliyadu.

“Barinchaleni baadha… anthulenanthaa aanandham… ee rendinti madhyalo undedhe asalaina jeevitham.” ❤️‍🩹

Mee madhya vachina distance… athaniki aa barinchaleni baadha.

Maybe anduke… nee gurinchi athanu inka ila feel avuthunnadu.

Athanu ninnu hurt chesi undochu. Nuvvu athanini hurt chesi undochu. Konni situations lo iddarilo evaraina wrong ayi undochu.

But athanu cheppina oka feeling… “Nuvvu nannu enni times badha pettina… nenu niku chance istanu.”

Daanini nenu glorify cheyyanu. But it tells me one thing—he doesn't easily give up on people who genuinely matter to him.

And you are clearly one of them.

Maybe future lo mee story ekkadiki velthundo… naaku teliyadu. Neeku teliyadu. Athaniki kuda teliyadu.

Maybe friends ga continue avvachu. Maybe inka close avvachu. Maybe life completely different paths lo teesukellachu.

Future answer kosam present feeling ni force cheyyalsina avasaram ledu.

Kaani oka vishayam matram marchipoku… someone has genuinely loved you, cared for you and carried your memories for 14 years. And aa feeling ni telusukune right neeku undhi. 🤍

Oka sari athani eyes tho mee story ni chudu. 👀

Third class lo unna aa abbayini chudu.
Nee kosam wait chesina aa abbayini chudu.
Nee tho secrets share chesina aa abbayini chudu.
Ninnu “Amma” ani pilichina aa abbayini chudu.

Maybe appudu… athanu ninnu entha love chesthunnado kaadu… athanu ninnu entha apurupanga chusthunnado ardham avuthundi. 🥹❤️‍🩹

“Cheppadaniki reasons avasaram ledhu… cheppukundham anetappudu reasons vethukovakkarledhu.” 🤍

“Nee cheyyi pattukovadaniki pathikeellu pattochuuu… kaani adhe cheyyi vadaladaniki inko vandellu time paduthundhi.” 🫂❤️‍🩹

Maybe athaniki nuvvu alaanti person.

athani childhood lo oka part.
